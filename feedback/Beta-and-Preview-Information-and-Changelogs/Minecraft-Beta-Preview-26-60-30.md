**Posted:** 06 October 2026

### **Information on Minecraft Preview and Beta:**

- These work-in-progress versions can be unstable and may not be representative of final version quality
- Minecraft Preview is available on Xbox, PlayStation, Windows, and iOS devices. More information can be found at [aka.ms/PreviewFAQ](https://aka.ms/PreviewFAQ)
- The beta is available on Android (Google Play). To join or leave the beta, see [aka.ms/JoinMCBeta](https://aka.ms/JoinMCBeta) for detailed instructions

It's time for another Preview and Beta release! For the full list of goodies, browse the changelog below. And as always, we’re keen to get your feedback on these new features at [feedback.minecraft.net](https://feedback.minecraft.net/), and you can report any bugs you find at [bugs.mojang.com](https://bugs.mojang.com/).

# Features and Bug Fixes

## Realms

- Major improvements have been made to world upload speed and reliability.

## Add-Ons

- Worlds that had achievements incorrectly disabled due to add-on use will have them re-enabled on first play ([MCPE-242586](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MCPE-242586))

## User Interface

- Fix visual stuttering with touch controls ([MCPE-243048](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MCPE-243048))
