Show of hands – any hand! – of who's been waiting for off-hand to come to Bedrock. Finally! Well, almost finally. We're still in testing, so you can try it out now and send us your feedback so we can get just right for the proper release next year. What will you hold in your off-hand first? Your pickaxe? A poisonous potato? Fireworks? Wait, that's already a thing. But now you can hold it there because you want to, not because it's more practical. And while the novelty of holding a nautilus shell or map in your off-hand might fade, that pales in comparison to the joy placing torches while digging up glorious, glorious gravel.

And as always, we’re keen to get your feedback on these new features at [feedback.minecraft.net](https://feedback.minecraft.net/), and you can report any bugs you find at [bugs.mojang.com](https://bugs.mojang.com/).

## Experimental Features

### Off-hand Parity

- Available with the Off-hand Parity experiment enabled:
  - You can now hold any block in your off-hand and place blocks directly from it.
  - You can now use items from your off-hand, including interactions such as stripping Logs with an Axe.
  - You can swap items between your main hand and off-hand.
    - On keyboard and mouse, press F by default.
    - On controller, the left face button now swaps items instead of opening your inventory.
  - You may use the new Shield button on touch controls to raise your Shield in off-hand.

## Features and Bug Fixes

#### Blocks

- Calcite Wall, Polished Calcite Wall, Polished Calcite Brick Wall and Calcite Pillar now only drop when mined with a Pickaxe

### Mobs

- Frostbite now has the same melee damage as Zombie ([MCPE-244145](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MCPE-244145))

### Sounds

- Splash Potions now play a sound again when landing ([MCPE-244256](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MCPE-244256))
