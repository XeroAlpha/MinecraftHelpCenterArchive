**Posted:** October 7, 2026

A new Minecraft Dungeons II update is available! The update is rolling out first on Steam and will reach Windows and XBOX X\|S soon.

- Fixed a variety of client and server crashes that could occur during gameplay
- Fixed a crash that could occur when the Prime Enchanter hit the floor with Lightning Slam ability in the same moment as a TNT explosion
- Fixed a crash that could occur when exiting an online game
- Fixed blurry visuals when the game was launched with Ultra graphics quality

The team is focused on additional fixes and improvements based on your reports. Please keep your feedback coming at [aka.ms/Dungeons2Feedback](https://aka.ms/Dungeons2Feedback) and report any bugs to [aka.ms/Dungeons2Bugs](https://aka.ms/Dungeons2Bugs)
