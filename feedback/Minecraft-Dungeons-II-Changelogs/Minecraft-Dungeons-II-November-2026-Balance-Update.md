**Posted:** November 16, 2026

Greetings, Heroes! We've published a game balance update to all platforms, introducing changes based on player feedback. No game update is required, and the changes will take effect the next time you login to the game.

## Balance Changes:[](https://dev.azure.com/dev-mc/Spicewood/_wiki/wikis/Spicewood.wiki?wikiVersion=GBwikiMaster&pagePath=/Release%20Branches/Dungeon%20II%20Changelogs/Changelogs%20for%20Review/November%202026%20Balance%20Update&pageId=42179&_a=edit&anchor=balance-changes%3A)

- Increased Echo Shard cap from **100 → 999**
- Increased Emerald cap from **99,999 → 999,999**
- Buffed the Corrupted Beacon artifact:
  - Increased damage **9 → 18**
  - Decreased time between hits **0.8 seconds → 0.3 seconds**
  - Increased movement speed **0.05 → 0.15**
  - Increased rotation speed **0.01 → 0.03**
  - Updated soul cost to be more constant during item usage **20 souls every 1 second → 6 souls every 0.3 seconds**
- Reduced Poison damage over time to players **15 seconds (1/2 full health) → 10 second (1/3 full health)**
- Reduced Soul Corrupted Witch boss health **1200 → 1000**
- Increased Emerald caps for maximum damage bonuses of Thrifty Pendant talisman from **7K/8K/9K** to **50K/200K/500K**

We're currently focused on additional improvements based on your feedback, as well as the first DLC: [The Humblers Awaken](https://www.minecraft.net/article/the-humblers-awaken)!
