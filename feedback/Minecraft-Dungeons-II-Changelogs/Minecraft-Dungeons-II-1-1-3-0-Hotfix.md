**Posted:** October 22, 2026

A new Minecraft Dungeons II update is available on all platforms, bringing stability improvements and other top blocking issues.

## Stability and Performance[](https://dev.azure.com/dev-mc/Spicewood/_wiki/wikis/Spicewood.wiki?wikiVersion=GBwikiMaster&pagePath=/Release%20Branches/Dungeon%20II%20Changelogs/Changelogs%20for%20Review/R2H3%20%252D%201.1.3.0%20Changelog&pageId=41861&_a=edit&anchor=stability-and-performance)

- Fixed several crashes that could occur during gameplay
- Fixed a hard lock that would occur when a couch co-op party returned to the main menu from the Overworld and the second player was kicked from the party when the attempting to create or select a Hero
- Improved login stability around error code "CH1"

## Items[](https://dev.azure.com/dev-mc/Spicewood/_wiki/wikis/Spicewood.wiki?wikiVersion=GBwikiMaster&pagePath=/Release%20Branches/Dungeon%20II%20Changelogs/Changelogs%20for%20Review/R2H3%20%252D%201.1.3.0%20Changelog&pageId=41861&_a=edit&anchor=items)

- Fixed artifact slots becoming unusable after being used in rapid succession ([MCD2-135](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MCD2/issues/MCD2-135))

## Quests[](https://dev.azure.com/dev-mc/Spicewood/_wiki/wikis/Spicewood.wiki?wikiVersion=GBwikiMaster&pagePath=/Release%20Branches/Dungeon%20II%20Changelogs/Changelogs%20for%20Review/R2H3%20%252D%201.1.3.0%20Changelog&pageId=41861&_a=edit&anchor=quests)

- Fixed the Prime Enchanter arena remaining lowered and preventing progression after leaving and rejoining a session ([MCD2-67](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MCD2/issues/MCD2-67))
- Fixed the quest marker not pointing to the correct locations during the "Wrath of the Sculkers" quest
- Fixed the quest marker not pointing to the correct location during the "Training with the Enchantsmith" quest ([MCD2-23](https://bugs.mojang.com/browse/MCD2/issues/MCD2-23))
- Fixed the quest journal displaying too many player icons during online sessions

## General[](https://dev.azure.com/dev-mc/Spicewood/_wiki/wikis/Spicewood.wiki?wikiVersion=GBwikiMaster&pagePath=/Release%20Branches/Dungeon%20II%20Changelogs/Changelogs%20for%20Review/R2H3%20%252D%201.1.3.0%20Changelog&pageId=41861&_a=edit&anchor=general)

- Updated the game's icon on Nintendo Switch and PlayStation 5, as well as in several places on XBOX and Windows
- Fixed an issue with opening the Deluxe Edition store page when playing on Nintendo Switch

The team is focused on additional fixes and improvements based on your reports.
