Welcome to Minecraft Feedback! This is where the community shares ideas, votes on favorites and tells us what they think of new Minecraft releases. Your creativity and passion help us make Minecraft even better, and we love seeing what you come up with!

For your feedback to have the biggest impact, it needs to be easy to find, read and discuss. That's what these guidelines are for. By posting on feedback.minecraft.net, you agree to follow the [Minecraft Community Standards](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/community-standards), the [Minecraft Feedback Terms of Service](../Gameplay-and-Support-Resources/Terms-of-Service.md) and these Posting Guidelines. If you don't agree with them, please don't use this site.

## Be kind and constructive

- Share your ideas and comments respectfully. Disagreeing is fine, as long as you're talking about the idea and not attacking the person who posted it.
- Be the kind of community member you'd like to meet.
- If you see something offensive, report it and don't respond to it. Our moderators will review it.

## Keep it focused

- This site is for ideas and feedback about Minecraft. Posts or comments that don't include any feedback, such as general questions or test posts, will be removed.
- Search before you post. A few keywords in the search bar are usually enough to find an existing thread. If your idea is already there, add your vote and join the conversation. Duplicate posts may be removed, and we'll point you to the main thread.

## Let the votes do the talking

- Use the voting system to support the ideas you like.
- Please don't post petitions or polls, and don't leave comments asking people to vote for another idea.

## Keep it on topic and free of spam

- Keep your posts about Minecraft and avoid comparing it to other platforms or manufacturers.
- No spam. That includes identical posts repeated over and over, ads, self-promotion, adding too many pictures, and heavy use of emojis or text formatting.
- No external links. Links to official Minecraft sites such as minecraft.net are fine.

## Please post in English

- To help our team review feedback consistently, please write your post in English or include an English translation. Posts in other languages will be removed, but you're welcome to post your idea again in English.

## Found a bug? It goes somewhere else

- The feedback team can't fix technical issues, so bug reports posted here will be removed. Please use [the bug tracker](https://bugs.mojang.com/) instead. 
- If you require help, head over to our [Help Center](https://help.minecraft.net/hc/en-us).

## Make it a place everyone can enjoy

Minecraft Feedback is for players of all backgrounds, so please keep your posts kind, honest, and appropriate for a wide audience. Content that breaks the Minecraft Community Standards will be removed, and the author may be suspended. This includes:

- Hate speech, harassment or bullying
- Threats, terrorism, violent extremism, or extreme violence and gore
- Nudity, pornography or sexual content of any kind
- Anything that exploits or puts children at risk
- Defamation, impersonation or false information
- Profanity, or content that promotes illegal drug use or underage drinking

#### You must also be 13 or older to post on Minecraft Feedback.

## What happens if a post breaks the rules

- Posts and comments that don't follow these guidelines may be edited, removed or moved to appropriate categories. 
- Serious or repeated violations can lead to a temporary or permanent suspension, without notice.
- If you create another account to get around a suspension, that account will be suspended too.

## Official Mojang posts

Posts from the Mojang Team usually have a *Pinned, Official reply* or *Official comment* tags. These are great places to share feedback on specific features and releases, so jump into the comments!

#### Thanks for helping keep Minecraft Feedback organized, and for being part of the community!
