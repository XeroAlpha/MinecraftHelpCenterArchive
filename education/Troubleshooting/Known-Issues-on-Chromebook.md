## Exported structure blocks don’t open in 3DViewer

<figure class="wysiwyg-table wysiwyg-table-align-left">
<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 50%" />
<col style="width: 50%" />
</colgroup>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: right; padding: 5px; vertical-align: top; width: 20%;"><strong>Description</strong></td>
<td style="padding: 5px">The .GLB files for structure blocks don’t open in other platforms when exported from Chromebook</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: right; padding: 5px; vertical-align: top;"><strong>Status</strong></td>
<td style="padding: 5px">No fix available</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: right; padding: 5px; vertical-align: top;"><strong>Workaround</strong></td>
<td style="padding: 5px"><p>Follow these steps as a workaround:</p>
<p>1.       On Chromebook, export the world.</p>
<p>2.       On the new platform,</p>
<p>a.       Import the world.</p>
<p>b.       Export the .GLB file.</p></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</figure>

 

## Proxy server support not available

<figure class="wysiwyg-table wysiwyg-table-align-left">
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: right; padding: 5px; vertical-align: top; width: 20%;"><strong>Description</strong></td>
<td style="padding: 5px">Proxy servers are not supported on Chromebook. If attempting to sign into Minecraft Education on a network using a proxy, you will see the Can’t Connect to the Service error</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: right; padding: 5px; vertical-align: top;"><strong>Status</strong></td>
<td style="padding: 5px">No fix available</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: right; padding: 5px; vertical-align: top;"><strong>Workaround</strong></td>
<td style="padding: 5px">Check your network isn’t using a proxy, for additional information see Troubleshoot the Can’t Connect to the Service Error article</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</figure>
