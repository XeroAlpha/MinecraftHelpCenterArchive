Minecoins are Minecraft’s virtual currency and can be used to purchase additional content from the [Minecraft Marketplace](./Buy-Minecoins-in-the-Minecraft-Marketplace.md). Starting October 27, 2026, new Minecoins purchased on Nintendo devices (Nintendo Switch or Switch 2) will be available to use only on those systems. Minecoins you already own at the time of the update will still be available, as explained below.

## How will my Minecoin wallets change?

When Minecraft launches on Nintendo devices, Minecoins owned before October 27, 2026, will move to your Legacy Wallet and remain available across compatible platforms. Minecoins purchased on a Nintendo device after launch will be added to your Nintendo Wallet. Here’s how each wallet works: 

### Legacy Wallet: existing and cross-platform Minecoins

- You won’t lose any Minecoins you already own, and you can still use existing Minecoins on any supported platforms where they were available before the change.
- When you make a purchase on a Nintendo device, Minecoins in your Legacy Wallet will be used before Minecoins in your Nintendo Wallet.
  - Example: If your Legacy Wallet contains 1,000 Minecoins and you purchase 500 more on a Nintendo device after launch, your displayed balance in the Marketplace will be 1,500 Minecoins. Any purchases will first use the 1,000 Minecoins in your Legacy Wallet, followed by the 500 Minecoins in your Nintendo Wallet.
- Minecoins in your Legacy Wallet do not expire. Future Minecoin charges and refills made on supported platforms will be added to that platforms wallet and shared across platforms where it is allowed. 

### Nintendo Wallet: new Minecoins purchased on Nintendo Switch systems

- Minecoins purchased on a Nintendo device after launch will be added to your Nintendo Wallet, which you can refill at any time.
- After you spend all the Minecoins in your Legacy Wallet, purchases on Nintendo devices will only use Minecoins from your Nintendo Wallet.
