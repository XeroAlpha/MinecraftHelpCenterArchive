To uninstall Minecraft Dungeons II, follow the steps below:

## Windows 11

[Uninstall or remove apps and programs in Windows - Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/uninstall-or-remove-apps-and-programs-in-windows-4b55f974-2cc6-2d2b-d092-5905080eaf98)

## Xbox

On Xbox, you can uninstall Minecraft Dungeons II by using the Xbox console’s Settings menu or the Xbox app on Windows, and if installed via Game Pass, also remove it from your Microsoft account’s Game Pass library.

### Uninstall on Xbox Series X\|S

1.  Press the **Xbox button** on your controller to open the guide.
2.  Go to **Profile & System \> Settings \> General \> Network settings \> Advanced settings \> Console settings**.
3.  Select **Manage games and apps**.
4.  Highlight **Minecraft Dungeons II** in the list.
5.  Press the **Menu button** (three dots) and choose **Uninstall**.
6.  Wait for confirmation that the game was removed.

Note: This will delete the game from your console’s storage. If you want to keep your save data, note that some Minecraft Dungeons II data is tied to your Microsoft account and may remain in the cloud.

### Uninstall via Xbox app on Windows

If you installed Minecraft Dungeons II via the Xbox app on your PC, follow these steps:

1.  **Sign in** with the same Microsoft account used on your Xbox.
2.  Open the **Xbox app** (search for it in the Start menu).
3.  In the left panel, click **Games & Apps**.
4.  Find **Minecraft Dungeons II** in the list.
5.  Right-click on it and select **Uninstall game**.
6.  Confirm the removal.

### Remove from Game Pass

1.  Go to [account.microsoft.com/services](https://account.microsoft.com/services) and sign in.
2.  Find **Minecraft Dungeons II** in your list.
3.  Click **Unsubscribe** to remove it from your Game Pass library.

## PlayStation 5

If you want to remove Minecraft Dungeons II from your PS5, you can do it directly from the console game library without affecting your saves or progress. Here’s how:

1.  From the PS5 home screen, scroll right and select **Game Library** to see all installed games.
2.  Use the search bar or scroll to locate **Minecraft Dungeons II**. Select the game and open its main menu.
3.  On your controller, press the **Options** button while the game is selected. Choose **Manage Game Content** from the Menu.
4.  In the **Manage Game Content** menu, you’ll see the base game, updates, and any DLCs. Highlight the content you want to uninstall and select **Delete** or **Uninstall**.
5.  Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm removal. The game will be removed from your library, but your save data will remain unless you manually delete it.

**Important Notes**

- Uninstalling the base game will remove all DLCs and updates. If you want to keep DLCs, only uninstall the extras.
- Your save data is stored separately from the game files. So your progress should remain intact unless you delete the save folder manually.
- If you reinstall later, you can redownload DLCs for free from the PlayStation Store if you use the same account.

**Tip:** If you want to free up storage, you can also uninstall the game entirely from the Game Library by selecting it, pressing Options, and choosing Delete. This will remove both the game and its save data.

## Nintendo Switch

If you want to remove Minecraft Dungeons II from your Nintendo Switch, you can do it directly from the console game library without affecting your saves or progress. Here’s how:

1.  Turn on your Nintendo Switch and go to the **Home menu**.
2.  Open **Settings** (the gear icon at the bottom of the screen).
3.  Select **Data Management** from the menu on the left.
4.  Select **Software** to see all of your installed games.
5.  Highlight **Minecraft Dungeons II** and press the **+** button (or select it from the list).
6.  Choose **Manage Software** (if prompted) and then **Delete Software**.
7.  Confirm by selecting **Delete** in the pop-up. The game’s icon will be removed from your **Home** menu.

Note: This process deletes the game software but does not remove your save data. Your progress is tied to your Nintendo Account and will be restored if you re-install the game.
