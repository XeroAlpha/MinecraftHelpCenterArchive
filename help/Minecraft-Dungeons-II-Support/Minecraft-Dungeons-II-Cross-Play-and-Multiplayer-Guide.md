# Multiplayer and Cross-Platform Play

Team up with friends across consoles and PCs. Create a multiplatform alliance and battle together with the cross-platform Minecraft Dungeons II. This allows for cross-platform multiplayer playing across Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation® 5, Windows PC, Xbox Series X\|S, and Steam. 

# Online Play  

Minecraft Dungeons II can be played online with 2-4 players. The game’s difficulty will change based on how many players are within the game, becoming more difficult with each friend that joins. You can also start an online game by yourself and have others join your quest for glory while your game is in progress. 

# How Do I Play with Friends

## Enable Multiplayer and Cross-Platform Play for Minecraft Dungeons II

To play online multiplayer and cross-platform play in Minecraft Dungeons II:

1.  Launch Minecraft Dungeons II.
2.  Open **Heroes** on the main menu.
3.  On the Heroes screen, select a Hero that is set to Online. You can toggle through All Heroes, Offline Heroes, and Online Heroes. If you don’t have a Hero that was created to play online, follow these steps:
    1.  Select **Create New Hero.**
    2.  On the next screen, you can choose to create a Hero to **Play Online** or **Play Offline**. To play solo, with friends online, or together on one device, you will need to choose Play Online.
    3.  To play offline or with friends on one device, you can select **Play Offline**. \
         

**Note:** If you are playing with an offline character, you will see a notification on the main menu that you are Playing Offline. In addition, the **Join a Party** option on the main menu will be grayed out, and you won’t be able to select it.

# How to Join a Party

## To Join a Friend's Session

There are a few ways that you can join a Party. The steps below will explain how to do so.

1.  Start Minecraft Dungeons II.
2.  From the Main Menu screen, look for the **Party** option at the bottom of the Menu.
3.  Open the **Party** session list.
4.  Find your friend's session on the session list. 
5.  Choose your friend's session and press **Select**. If the session isn’t listed, and you have your friend’s party code, select **Play with Friends** from the Party Menu and enter the party code to Join Party.

OR

1.  Start Minecraft Dungeons II.
2.  On the Main Menu screen, select **Join a Party**.
3.  Select **Enter Code** if you already have a friend’s party code to join their existing session. Select **Matchmaking** if you would like to get matched with other random players.

## To Invite Friends to Join Your Game

1.  From the Main Menu screen, open the **Party** menu.
2.  Choose the friends you want to invite to your session, select **More Actions**, then select **Invite**. 
3.  They'll need to accept the request before joining your game. 

**Note:** If you want to start a Private Session, choose **Party Settings\>Friends Party OR Private Party** so only invited friends can join.

You can also access the Party Settings from within the game via the Map button. 

## Why Can't I Join a Party

If you're unable to join a party in Minecraft Dungeons II, it's usually due to one of several factors - from matchmaking rules to network or account settings.

## Multiplayer Modes and Party Codes

Minecraft Dungeons II supports four ways to play together:

- Couch Co-Op (local split screen)
- Play with Friends (from your friends list)
- Matchmaking (auto-paired strangers)
- Party Codes (share a code to meet up)

If you're trying to join a friend's session but it's not working, you may need to use a **Party Code** instead of adding them as a friend, since matchmaking doesn't let you choose who you get.

## Version and Cross-Play Requirements

All players must be on the same game version (latest update) and have cross-play enabled. Version mismatches or outdated builds can block connections. For cross-platform play (PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch), each player needs an active subscription to access online play.

- Xbox - Game Pass Ultimate
- PlayStation - PlayStation Plus
- Nintendo Switch - Nintendo Switch Online

## Account and Settings Restrictions

- Make sure you're in an Online Game (not offline) when inviting friends.
- Check your Microsoft account privacy settings and Xbox/PlayStation online safety settings to ensure multiplayer is enabled.

# How Do I Create a Party Code

1.  **Launch Minecraft Dungeons II** and go to the **Main Menu.**
2.  From the Main Menu screen, look for the **Party** option at the bottom of the Menu.
3.  Open the **Party** session list.
4.  On the top left side of the screen, you will see Your Party Code. 
5.  Share this code with your friends, who can enter it into the **Join with Code** option to connect directly.

**Important Notes:**

- **Cross-Play supported** - Friends on different platforms (PC, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch) can join using the same party code.
- **Mixed Multiplayer** - You can combine local couch co-op players with online friends via the same party code.
- **Code Expiration** - Party codes are temporary and expire when the party ends. If the host leaves, the party leader designation will transfer to another player in the party.
- **Privacy** - Only share your code with people you trust as *anyone* with the code can join your game.

If you want to keep your party private, disable matchmaking in the lobby settings so only players with your code can join.

# Why Doesn't My Party Code Work

If your **Party Code** isn't working in Minecraft Dungeons II, it's usually due to one of a few common issues - many of which also affect friend sessions, since Party Codes and matchmaking use the same connection system.

## Check All Players' Subscriptions

All players must have an active online service subscription. If any player's subscription is expired or inactive, the connection will fail.

- Xbox - Game Pass Ultimate
- PlayStation - PlayStation Plus
- Nintendo Switch - Nintendo Switch Online

## Match the Game Version

For cross-play, everyone must be on the same version of Minecraft Dungeons II. Version mismatches can block joining, even if both are updated.

- On PC, check in-game settings or Steam/Microsoft Store.
- On consoles, update the game to the latest patch.

## Privacy and Account Settings

On Xbox, make sure your account is set to allow joining multiplayer games, and that your age/family settings aren't blocking access. On other platforms, check privacy settings to allow online sessions.

Session Setup

- The host must start the game and reach the Camp after completing the tutorial.
- The joining player must refresh the friend list until they see the host's session.
- For party codes, make sure you're entering the code in the correct menu (usually under **Join A Party\>Play With Friends** or **Party\>Play With Friends**). 

## Temporary Service Issues

If the game shows **"service not available"** or gets stuck in loading, it could be the result of a server outage. Wait a few hours and try again.

## Mixing Couch Co-Op and Online Players

You can combine two local players with two online players, but all must be in the same session.

# Why Can't I Promote the Party Leader

In Minecraft Dungeons II, the party leader role is tied to the Party Finder system. If you're unable to promote a Party Leader, it could be due to one of the following issues:

## You Must Be the One Who Starts the Party

In the Party Finder, only the person who creates the party can make themselves or another player the leader. If you're not the creator, you won't be able to promote anyone. You could join an existing party and then request a leader change from the party owner.

## Leader Role is Tied to the Party Finder Queue

The "party leader" status is specific to the Party Finder dungeon queue, not the main dungeon finder. If you try to queue into a dungeon as a leader but the party isn't properly set up in Party Finder, the system may not allow the promotion.

## Check Party Size and Rules

Party Finder has a limit of up to four players, and all members must be online and in the same session. If someone is offline or the party is incomplete, the leader role can't be applied.
