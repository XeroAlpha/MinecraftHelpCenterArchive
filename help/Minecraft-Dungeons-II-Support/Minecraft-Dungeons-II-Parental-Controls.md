You can buy Minecraft Dungeons II for a child account through the official Minecraft store using the child’s Microsoft account and then apply Microsoft’s parental controls to limit spending and content.

1.  [Create a Microsoft child account](../Resources-for-Families/Set-Up-Microsoft-Family-Groups-for-Parental-Controls-in-Minecraft.md) for your child (or use an existing child account) with the child’s name and date of birth. This account will be the one that purchases the game.
2.  Choose either the **Standard Edition**, which includes the base game, two hero skins, the Twisted Cape, and the Twisted Chicken Pet, or the **Deluxe Edition** which includes everything in the Standard Edition PLUS future DLCs, extra skins, and pets. Buy Minecraft Dungeons II through a supported platform here:
    1.  [Minecraft.net store](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/store/minecraft-dungeons-ii)
    2.  [Xbox console store](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/minecraft-dungeons-ii/9NSN56SL5HC0)
    3.  [Other participating platforms](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/store/minecraft-dungeons-ii) (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, Steam)
    4.  You can also get Minecraft Dungeons II via Xbox Game Pass if your child account is part of a family share and you have an active subscription. 
3.  Complete payment by following the purchase flow. The purchase will be billed to the Microsoft account payment method. 

**Note:** If you want to avoid direct payment by the child, set up **Microsoft’s parental controls** (see below) before completing your purchase.

## To set up Parental Controls

You can set up parental controls for your child’s account which will allow you to create the following limitations:

- [Managing Spending for Child Accounts in Minecraft Marketplace](../Buying-Marketplace-Content/Managing-Spending-for-Child-Accounts-in-Minecraft-Marketplace.md)
- [Managing Multiplayer Game Access for a Child Account in Minecraft](../Account-Settings/Managing-Multiplayer-Game-Access-for-a-Child-Account-in-Minecraft.md)
- [Allow a Microsoft Child Account to Redeem Codes for Minecraft](../Account-Settings/Allow-a-Microsoft-Child-Account-to-Redeem-Codes-for-Minecraft.md)
- [Managing Child Account Social Settings Using Xbox Settings Online](../Account-Settings/Managing-Child-Account-Social-Settings-Using-Xbox-Settings-Online.md)
- [Managing Child Account Social Settings Using the Xbox Family App](../Account-Settings/Managing-Child-Account-Social-Settings-Using-the-Xbox-Family-App.md)
- [Change a Child Account’s Privacy & Safety Permissions for Minecraft Realms Stories](../Minecraft-Realms-Stories/Change-a-Child-Account-s-Privacy-Safety-Permissions-for-Minecraft-Realms-Stories.md)

**Note:** If your child account is part of a family share, you can give them access to Minecraft Dungeons II via Game Pass without needing to buy it.  \
 \
Make sure to check platform rules as on Xbox, PlayStation, and Nintendo Switch as you may need to link the child account to your main account for purchase approval.
