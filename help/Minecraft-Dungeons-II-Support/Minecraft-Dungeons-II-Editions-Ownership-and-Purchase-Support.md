# Choose an Edition

## Standard Edition 

Minecraft Dungeons II Standard Edition includes the full base game (campaign, co-op, gear progression, new biomes), as well as two hero skins included in the base game. 

## Deluxe Edition

Minecraft Dungeons II Deluxe Edition includes everything in the Standard Edition, and you'll also get the Soul Cape, the Blub Pet, four exclusive Hero Skins (Valorie, Kellen, Flores, Ren) in addition to the two skins included in the base game, and access to two post-launch DLC packs when they become available.

## Deluxe Upgrade

If you already own Minecraft Dungeons II Standard Edition, you can upgrade to the Deluxe Edition to add more content to your game. The cost to upgrade is \$20 and it will automatically add both future DLCs (DLC 1 and DLC 2 as they become available) and exclusive cosmetics.

## Game Pass Ownership

Minecraft Dungeons II will be available on Xbox Game Pass on day one, September 29, 2026. You'll be able to play Minecraft Dungeons II through your subscription without any additional costs. 

## Play on Xbox and Windows with One Purchase

Minecraft Dungeons II supports [Xbox Play Anywhere](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/xbox-play-anywhere).

When you purchase the game digitally through the [Microsoft Store on Xbox or Windows](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/store/minecraft-dungeons-ii/9p5786pjb9rp), you can play on both Xbox consoles and Windows PCs without purchasing the game a second time.

To access your game on another supported device:

1.  Sign in using the same Microsoft account used for your purchase.
2.  Install the game from your library.
3.  Launch and play on your preferred platform.

Visit [Minecraft Dungeons II](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/about-dungeons-ii) on Minecraft.net to learn more.

# DLC Access

It's always better to play together, whether or not everyone owns downloadable content (DLC). Access to DLC content depends on who starts the session and how the session is joined.

## DLC Owners

- Can host DLC sessions that friends can join.
- Can join any sessions through matchmaking, whether the DLC is enabled or not.
- Won't be able to play DLC content when playing in a non-DLC session (shown with a locked icon).

## Non-DLC Owners

- Can join friends' DLC sessions through a Party Code or invitation.
- Can't join DLC sessions through matchmaking.
- Some DLC progress may be saved and can be continued in other DLC sessions or when purchasing DLC (shown with an unlocked icon).

# Physical vs Digital Purchases

If you want a physical copy of Minecraft Dungeons II, the Deluxe Edition is the only way to get it. The physical copy includes the full base game, as well as the Soul Cape, Blub Pet, four exclusive Hero Skins (Valorie, Kellen, Flores, Ren), and access to two post-launch DLC packs when they become available.

# Gift Purchases

You can give Minecraft Dungeons II as a gift through Minecraft.net. Follow the steps below to purchase it as a gift for someone today!

**Note:** Gifting will only be available after launch on September 29, 2026.

## How to Send a Gift on Minecraft.net

1.  Navigate to [Minecraft.net](https://www.minecraft.net/) and select **Games** from the top menu.
2.  Select the game you'd like to purchase from the menu on the left and click Buy Minecraft Dungeons II on the right side of the screen.
3.  Select the platform you'd like to purchase the game for. The default is PC but if you’d like to purchase Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, or Steam, select from **Other Platforms** below.
4.  Click **Buy as Gift** and sign in to your Microsoft account if prompted.
5.  Enter the recipient's email address in the **Sending Gift To** field and enter your name in the **Sender Name** field on the Send Gift screen.
6.  Click Next.
7.  Add a payment method or select an existing one.
8.  Check all the details on the confirmation screen and select **Buy as Gift** to complete the purchase.

## How to Send a Gift on the Microsoft Store

1.  Navigate to the [Buy Minecraft Dungeons II](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/minecraft-dungeons-ii/9p5786pjb9rp) page.
2.  Click **Sign In** on the upper right-hand corner of the page and sign in with the Microsoft account you want to use to buy the gift if you haven't already. 
3.  Choose the game edition you want to buy as a gift.
4.  Click the **Gift** icon button.
5.  Enter the email address of the person you want to receive the gift in the **Sending Gift** field.
6.  Enter your **Sender Name** and click **Next**.
7.  Add a payment method or select an exiting one.
8.  Click the **Buy As Gift** button to complete your purchase.

You can track your order at [account.microsoft.com/billing/orders](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/orders). 

## How to Redeem Minecraft Dungeons II as a Gift

If you received Minecraft Dungeons II as a gift, follow the steps below to redeem your gift code, download, and install the game. Any email can receive a Minecraft code, but you'll need to sign in to the Microsoft account you want to use to play Minecraft Dungeons II when you redeem the code.

1.  Open the email that includes your Minecraft gift code.
2.  Note the 25-character code provided in the email.
3.  Click the **Redeem** your gift button in your gift email or visit the [gift code redemption page](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/redeem) in your Microsoft account. 

**Note:** You can also redeem gift codes at [Minecraft.net/redeem](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/redeem).

1.  Sign in to the Microsoft account that you want to use to play Minecraft.
2.  Enter your code into the redemption box if prompted and click Next. If you visit the redeem page directly from the email, you will skip this step.
3.  Check the details of your gift and click the **Confirm** button.
4.  Follow the link on the confirmation screen to install Minecraft or visit [How to install the Minecraft Launcher](../Minecraft-Launcher-Support/Download-and-Install-the-Minecraft-Launcher.md) to learn about installing Minecraft for Windows, Mac, or Linux.

If you have purchased a Minecraft Dungeons II gift code for another platform, please follow the steps below for the appropriate platform:

- [Redeeming on Xbox One](https://support.xbox.com/help/subscriptions-billing/redeem-codes-gifting/redeem-prepaid-codes)
- [Redeeming on Nintendo Switch](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/22429/~/how-to-redeem-a-download-code-on-nintendo-switch-eshop#:~:text=%20How%20to%20Redeem%20a%20Download%20Code%20on,with%20a%20limited%20version%20of%20Nintendo...%20More%20)

# "I Purchased the Game, but I Cannot Access It"

If you purchased Minecraft Dungeons II but can’t access it, the issue is usually because of an account mismatch. Here’s how to troubleshoot it:

## Confirm the correct account

Minecraft Dungeons II requires a Microsoft account for login and license verification.

1.  Check that the Microsoft account you used to buy the game is also signed into: \
    - Xbox App \
    - Microsoft Store \
    - Minecraft Launcher
2.  Sign out of all of these apps, then sign back in with the same Microsoft account.
3.  Go to [account.microsoft.com/billing/orders](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/orders) to confirm the purchase is linked to that account. \
     

# How Can I Get a Refund

Each store that sells Minecraft Dungeons II has its own return policy. Please visit the store where you purchased your Minecraft Dungeons II game for return and refund information.

- Minecraft.net, Microsoft Store, or Xbox - [Microsoft Refund Policy](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/subscriptions-billing/buy-games-apps/refund-orders)
- Nintendo - [Nintendo Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.nintendo.com/us/returns-exchanges/)
- PlayStation - [PlayStation Refund Policy](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/ps-store-refund-request/)
- Steam - [Steam Refund Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds)

Generally, Minecraft games purchased from Minecraft.net or the Microsoft/Xbox Store are eligible for a refund within 14 days of the purchase.

Request a Refund from the Microsoft Store

1.  Visit your [Microsoft order history](https://account.microsoft.com/billing/orders) and sign in using your Microsoft account.
2.  Click the **Request a Return** option next to any item you wish to return. Only items eligible for return will display this option.
3.  Select a refund option to complete the refund process.
