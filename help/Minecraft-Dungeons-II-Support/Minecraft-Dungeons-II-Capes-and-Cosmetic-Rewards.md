Minecraft Dungeons II includes several capes and cosmetic rewards, each with its own eligibility requirements and availability. Learn how the Hero, Twisted, Soul, and Corrupted Creeper capes are earned, when rewards are granted, and whether they can also be used in Minecraft: Java Edition and Minecraft: Bedrock Edition.

# Available Capes and Rewards

## Hero Cape

The Hero Cape is a reward for players who have played Minecraft Dungeons and then play Minecraft Dungeons II using the same Microsoft account. 

### Eligibility

To qualify for the Hero Cape, a player must:

1.  Have Minecraft Dungeons save data associated with your Microsoft account.
2.  Play Minecraft Dungeons II while signed in to the same Microsoft account.

Purchasing Minecraft Dungeons is not strictly required. Players may qualify through Game Pass, local co-op participation, or another eligible play session that created qualifying save data.

### Grant timing

The Hero Cape is granted after launching Minecraft Dungeons II and the game detects qualifying Minecraft Dungeons activity on the same Microsoft account. Processing may take some time.

### Promotion Period

The Hero Cape promotion remains available through December 31, 2026, so players can still complete the requirements after launch.

### Availability in Minecraft

After the Hero Cape is granted in Minecraft Dungeons II, the Hero Cape also becomes available in Minecraft: Java Edition and Minecraft: Bedrock Edition when the player signs in with the same Microsoft account. 

 

## Twisted Cape (Pre-Order Bonus)

The Twisted Cape is a reward included with qualifying Minecraft Dungeons II pre-orders. 

### Eligibility

The Twisted Cape is included with qualifying Minecraft Dungeons II pre-orders placed during the eligible pre-order period, which expires on September 28, 2026.

### Grant and Activation

After Minecraft Dungeons II launches on September 29, 2026, complete these steps:

1.  Sign in to Minecraft Dungeons II using the Microsoft account associated with your pre-order.
2.  Confirm that your pre-order rewards have been granted in-game on the Collectibles screen.
3.  Sign in to Minecraft: Java Edition and/or Minecraft: Bedrock Edition using the same Microsoft 

### Availability in Minecraft

After the entitlement is granted, the Twisted Cape is also available in Minecraft: Java Edition and Minecraft: Bedrock Edition on the same Microsoft account. Processing may take some time.

 

## Soul Cape (Deluxe Edition)

The Soul Cape is included with the Minecraft Dungeons II Deluxe Edition.

### Eligibility

The Soul Cape is included with the Minecraft Dungeons II Deluxe Edition.

### Grant timing

The Soul Cape is granted as part of the Deluxe Edition content package after ownership is validated in Minecraft Dungeons II.

### Availability in Minecraft

The Soul Cape is exclusive to Minecraft Dungeons II.

 

# Promotional Capes

## Corrupted Creeper Cape

The Corrupted Creeper Cape is a promotional reward distributed through selected community and marketing events.

### Eligibility

Eligibility is determined by the rules of each promotion. Distribution may take place through:

- In-person events
- Other promotional programs announced by Minecraft

### Grant timing

Grant timing varies by promotion. Players should follow the rules and redemption instructions provided with the promotion they participated in.

### Availability in Minecraft

Availability outside Minecraft Dungeons II may vary. The applicable campaign details will specify whether the reward is supported in Minecraft: Java Edition or Minecraft: Bedrock Edition.

 

# Future Promotional Capes

Additional capes may be offered through events, partnerships, creator activities, or other limited-time promotions. Eligibility requirements, grant timing, redemption instructions, and supported games will be published for each promotion.

# Troubleshooting Missing Capes

## Microsoft account mismatch

Cape eligibility and grant processing are tied to the Microsoft account used when playing or redeeming content. All required activities must occur on the same Microsoft account.

## Minecraft Dungeons activity was not linked

On some platforms, signing into a Microsoft account is optional. If Minecraft Dungeons activity was completed without signing in, the activity may not be eligible for Hero Cape rewards.

## Grant processing is still underway

Reward verification and entitlement processing may take time after qualifying actions are completed.

## The required game has not been launched

Some rewards are not visible until you've launched Minecraft Dungeons II, and the entitlement has been validated. For cross-game rewards, you may also need to sign in to Minecraft: Java Edition for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition on the same Microsoft account.
