Accounts Required to Play Minecraft Dungeons II Online Multiplayer

## Windows 11

- Microsoft account

## Xbox Series X/S

- Xbox account
- Xbox Game Pass Essential, Platinum, Premium, or Ultimate subscription is required for online console multiplayer.

## PlayStation 5

- PlayStation Plus subscription is required for online multiplayer.

## Nintendo Switch

- Nintendo account
- Nintendo Switch Online membership is required for online multiplayer

## Steam

- Steam account

**Note:** For cross-platform progression, you’ll need to link your Microsoft account.

# How Do I Link an Account

Some Minecraft experiences allow you to connect accounts across platforms and services. Linking accounts can enable features such as cross-platform progression and access to account-based content.

# What Is Account Linking?

Account linking connects your platform account to another account used by Minecraft services.

This allows certain profile information and account-based content to be associated with the linked account experience. 

# Why Link an Account?

Depending on the game and platform, linking may enable:

- Cross-progression features
- Access to profile-based rewards
- Synchronization of supported content

## Cross-Platform Linking on PlayStation and Nintendo Switch

For cross-platform progression on PlayStation and Nintendo Switch, you’ll need to link your Microsoft account to your platform account in Minecraft Dungeons II.

1.  Launch Minecraft Dungeons II.
2.  If you have never linked your account before, you will be given the option to do so.
3.  Scan the QR code or visit: <https://microsoft.com/link>
4.  Enter the code provided on the platform you are linking to.
5.  Follow the linking prompts to link your account.

## Cross-Platform Linking on Steam

For cross-platform progression on Steam, you’ll need to link your Microsoft account to your platform account in Minecraft Dungeons II.

1.  Launch Minecraft Dungeons II.
2.  Go to Settings and you’ll be prompted to link your account if there is no account linked under Microsoft Account.
3.  Follow the linking prompts to link your account.

# How Do I Unlink an Account

## Unlinking on PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, and Steam

If you want to unlink your Microsoft account from Minecraft Dungeons II, follow the steps below:

1.  Go to Settings. If there is a Microsoft account linked to the account, you will be given the option to unlink it.
2.  Follow the prompts to unlink your account.

Note: Unlinking your account will allow you to keep your progress locally, but you will no longer be able top pick up where you left off on another platform.

# What Happens When I Unlink an Account

Unlinking removes the connection between accounts.

After unlinking:

- Some profile-based content, like roaming cosmetics, may no longer be available on that account connection and will return to the platform where they were claimed.
- You may need to relink the original account to access certain rewards.
- Progress and content availability can depend on where the content is stored. 

# Before You Unlink

We recommend:

- Confirming which account is currently linked.
- Verifying which account contains your rewards and progression.
- Reviewing any warnings shown during the unlink process.

# I'm Missing Content After Unlinking

If content is missing after unlinking:

1.  Verify the correct account is linked.
2.  Sign out and back in.
3.  Restart the game.
4.  Check whether the content was originally obtained on a different account.

If the issue continues, contact Minecraft Support.

# Switching Accounts

**Switching accounts in Minecraft Dungeons II affects progress, character ownership, and cross-play depending on whether you're using online multiplayer or local multiplayer.**

# What Happens if You Use the Wrong Account

If you accidentally log into Minecraft Dungeons II with the wrong Microsoft/Xbox account, you can usually sign out and switch accounts, but in some cases, you may need to remove saved credentials or refresh the in-game login. 

**Note:** Always double-check which Microsoft account you’re signing in to before launching the game and remove old credentials if you think the wrong account is stored. This will prevent the game from auto-selecting the wrong profile.

## Sign out of the wrong account in-game

1.  On console or PC, open the game’s Settings \> Online Play (or Accessibility on some platforms) and look for a Sign Out option.
2.  This will log you out of the Microsoft/Xbox account currently linked to the game.

## Remove saved credentials

If you can’t see a sign-out option or the game keeps prompting for the wrong account:

1.  On Windows, open Windows Credential Manager and remove any stored Xbox/Microsoft accounts.
2.  On PC, you can also sign out of the Xbox app and Microsoft Store.

## Refresh the in-game login browser

If the game still forces the old account, you can delete the cached login data:

1.  On Windows, go to %LocalAppData%\Dungeons II\Saved and delete the webcache folder.
2.  This clears the in-game browser cache used for Microsoft/Xbox sign-in, so you can choose a different account. 

## Switch accounts before launching

If you have multiple Microsoft accounts, sign into the correct one in the Xbox app or Microsoft Store before starting the game.

On PC, you can also add both accounts in the Minecraft Launcher’s account list so you can switch without re-entering credentials.

**Note:** Minecraft Dungeons II and its DLC cannot be transferred to another Microsoft account. If you bought the game on one account, that account must be used to play. If you used the wrong account, you may need to keep both accounts active or request a refund and repurchase the game on the correct account.

# Why is Content Available on One Platform but Not Another

Minecraft Dungeons II DLC is licensed separately for each platform, so you must buy it on the same platform you want to play on. It does not automatically transfer between consoles, PC, or other devices. 

## Cross-Play vs. Cross-DLC

Cross-Play lets you play with friends on other platforms, but DLC is still platform-specific unless the game explicitly supports “Cross-DLC” (which Minecraft Dungeons II currently does not).

In multiplayer, only the host needs to own the DLC for that session’s DLC missions/items to be available to all players. However, if you want to access DLC content on your own account outside of hosting, you must own it on that platform.
