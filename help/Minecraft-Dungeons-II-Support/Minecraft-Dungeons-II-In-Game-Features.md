# Enchantment Books

Enchantment books are used to enchant your items at the Enchantsmith. You'll need Enchantment Points to use them!

Collect enchantment books by completing quests and exploring dungeons and rifts.

# Pets

Pets are companions that you can equip, and that help you in battle with their unique looks and charisma, even if they don't directly participate in combat.

You can obtain pets from different editions of the game and through special promotions.

# Talismans

Talismans can be equipped to grant potent passive abilities, and you can have a maximum of three talismans at the same time.

They will gain Experience with you and become more powerful the longer you have them equipped.

Make sure to switch between your talismans to get them to max level to be ready for every type of challenge!

# Artifacts

Artifacts are powerful items that give unique abilities when equipped. You can have a maximum of three artifacts equipped, and you use them by pressing the buttons they are assigned to.

Using an artifact costs souls and many artifacts have cooldown periods between uses.

Different artifacts work in different ways; some are one-shot that trigger directly, some are channeling continuously, and some charge a big effect at the end. Use them wisely!

# Multiplayer Progression

Progress related to quests, level, and item drops will not be saved for you if the party leader has significantly higher progress than yours. Quests marked with this icon won't save progress. You will still be able to experience all the game features together.

You can play with a friend with similar progression as yours, matchmake, or play solo to advance your progression.

# Echo Shards

An echo shard is a resource dropped by defeated soul corrupted mobs and bosses, which can be used to apply enchantments to gear at an Enchantsmith. Merchants are also upgraded by using echo shards.

# Emeralds

An emerald is a resource used to purchase and upgrade items. They are dropped from chests and defeated mobs. You can also use emeralds with a blacksmith to upgrade gear to higher levels.

# Enchantment Points

An enchantment point is a resource used to apply enchantments to gear. They are gained by leveling up. Your Hero will start with no enchantment points and will gain one per level up to a maximum of 99. Enchanted books can be applied to gear by using an enchantment point at an enchantsmith.

# Food

Food can be dropped by artifacts, chests, and defeated mobs, and are auto-consumable items that instantly heal your Hero when used.
