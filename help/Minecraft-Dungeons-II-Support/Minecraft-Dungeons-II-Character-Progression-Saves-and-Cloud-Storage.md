# How Is Progress Saved

Progress is automatically saved every five minutes and during key events.

Progress can also be saved when you access the Map screen and scroll to System at the top of the screen. You can select **Save and exit to main menu** or **Save and quit to desktop** to save your progress.

Online vs. Offline Characters

## Understanding Online and Offline Characters

Your character progression is tied to your account and can be accessed across supported platforms when you're signed in with the same profile. 

## Playing Online

Whether you're connected to online services, or playing offline, your character progress is associated with your account. This allows you to continue playing on other supported devices using the same profile.

Only one active game session can be connected to your account at a time. If you sign in on another device, you'll be prompted to sign out of your current session.

## Playing Offline

Offline characters are stored both online and offline during play. Your progress is shared across devices when you use the same account. When you reconnect, your progress may synchronize with your online profile.

## Using Multiple Devices

To help ensure your progress is available across devices:

- Sign in with the same account everywhere you play. If you are using a non-Microsoft account, you should link your platform account to a MSA account.
- Connect to online services whenever possible.
- Allow synchronization to complete before switching to another device.
- Avoid playing offline on multiple devices at the same time.

## Missing Progress

If you play offline on multiple devices, different versions of your character progress can exist. In some cases, progress made on one device may not appear on another.

For the best experience, reconnect to online services and allow synchronization to complete before continuing your game on another device.

## Need Help?

If you're experiencing missing progress or save synchronization issues, contact Support and include the following:

- The platform(s) you're playing on.
- Whether you were playing online or offline.
- When the issue occurred.
- A description of the missing progress.

# Cloud Saves

If you have a hero that you have put a lot of hard work into building up in Minecraft Dungeons II, you should be able to continue using them even if you are playing the game on a different platform. Luckily, Minecraft Dungeons II has cloud saves.

## How to Upload a Hero to the Cloud in Minecraft Dungeons II

When **Cloud Save** is **ON**, your offline heroes’ progress is synced with the cloud. In the event of a conflict, the system will select the appropriate save across devices and sessions.

Turn this **OFF** to prevent any automatic cloud selection or syncing. This can avoid unintended overwrites or conflicts when multiple devices use the same character at the same time or when you haven’t had a chance to sync.

With this setting OFF, the game will only store your offline heroes locally, and you will not be able to access progress from other devices.

**Note:** this will only impact offline heroes; online heroes will always be synced to the cloud.

## How to Download a Hero from the Cloud in Minecraft Dungeons II 

After a Hero has been uploaded to the cloud in Minecraft Dungeons II, it will sync with another platform if you are signed into the same account and have **Cloud Saves** turned **ON**.

**Note:** If you are logged into one platform and then sign into another, you will receive the following message:

**Error code: 0048 **

**Too Many Devices:** You were logged out from this session because your account logged in on another device. 

Your Hero and all of their gear will become available when the download is completed.

# Character Progression

Minecraft Dungeons II saves character progression through a combination of local save files and optional cloud saves via your platform account, allowing cross-platform progression when set up manually.

## Local save files

Each character’s data is stored locally on your device in a **.dat file** inside your user’s save file. On Windows, for example, the path is:

%USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\Mojang Studios\Dungeons\\USER_ID\>\Characters\\character\>.dat

These files contain all progression data, including items, enchantments, mission unlocks, camp merchants, and more. If you uninstall the game or lose access to your local save data, you can back it up from this folder and restore it later.

## Cloud Saves

Minecraft Dungeons II supports cloud saves so you can sync characters on all platforms.

### How it works: 

1.  Launch Minecraft Dungeons II.
2.  Open **Heroes** on the main menu.
3.  On the Heroes screen, select a Hero that is set to Online. You can toggle through All Heroes, Offline Heroes, and Online Heroes. If you don’t have a Hero that was created to play online, follow these steps:
    1.  Select **Create New Hero.**
    2.  On the next screen, you can choose to create a Hero to **Play Online** or **Play Offline**. To play solo, with friends online, or together on one device, you will need to choose Play Online.
    3.  To play offline or with friends on one device, you can select **Play Offline**. \
         

**Note:** If you are playing with an offline character, you will see a notification on the main menu that you are Playing Offline. In addition, the **Join a Party** option on the main menu will be grayed out, and you won’t be able to select it. 

**You will not be able to change the Online/Offline setting after creating your character, so make sure to choose wisely when creating them.**

# How Do I Access Saves on Another Device

You can use Minecraft Dungeons II’s Cloud Save feature to download your Heroes to another device if you are signed into a shared account.
