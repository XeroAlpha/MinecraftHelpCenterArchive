Minecraft Dungeons II launches September 29, 2026. If you pre-order the game by September 28, 2026, you'll receive limited-time bonus cosmetics when the game launches.

# Choose an Edition

## Standard Edition

Pre-order the Standard Edition, which includes the base game, and get these pre-order bonus cosmetics: two additional Hero Skins, the Twisted Cape, and the Twisted Chicken Pet.

## Deluxe Edition

Pre-order the Deluxe Edition, which includes everything in the Standard Edition, and you'll also get the Soul Cape, the Blub Pet, four additional Hero Skins, and access to two post-launch DLC packs when they become available.

# Pre-Order Rewards

You can only get the pre-order rewards, which will be delivered after the game launches on September 29, 2026, for qualifying purchases made during the pre-order period. They are created for Minecraft Dungeons II and cannot be used in Minecraft: Bedrock Edition, Minecraft: Java Edition, or other Minecraft games.

## Where to Find Your Cosmetics

After the content is granted on launch day, September 29, 2026, open the in-game inventory and select the Collectibles tab to find your capes, pets, and Hero Skins.

## Platform and Account Rules

Pre-order and Deluxe Edition content is tied to the platform where it was purchased. Connecting a Steam account and a Microsoft account does not make the rewards available on every platform.

For digital purchases, the content is automatically granted to the account that completes the pre-order. If the game is intended for a child or another player, purchase or gift it for the account that should own the game and rewards; an existing pre-order generally cannot be transferred between accounts.

# Where You Can Play

Minecraft Dungeons II will be available on Xbox Series X\|S, Windows PC, Steam, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2. Pre-order rewards are also available through Steam for qualifying purchases.

## Nintendo Switch Versions

Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 are separate platform versions. Before purchasing from a retailer, make sure that the product listing supports the Nintendo Switch model you plan to use. A Nintendo Switch 2 version should not be assumed to work on a Nintendo Switch or Nintendo Switch Lite unless the retailer or platform listing specifically says it is compatible.

# Game Pass, Upgrades, and DLC

## Game Pass

Minecraft Dungeons II will be available with Game Pass on launch day. Game Pass access is not a pre-order purchase, so it does not include the pre-order bonus cosmetics. If a store says you already own the game, your active Game Pass entitlement may be the reason.

## Upgrading to Deluxe

Players who purchase the Standard Edition can upgrade to the Deluxe Edition after purchase. Any available upgrade price, discount, or purchase option is set by the store where the game was purchased.

The Deluxe Edition includes two specified post-launch DLC packs. Pricing for any other future content will be announced separately if additional content is offered.

# Physical and Gift Purchases

Pre-order content for physical editions depends on the retailer. Before buying, check the product listing or ask the retailer whether the physical copy includes a pre-order code and how that code will be delivered. Digital purchases grant qualifying content directly to the purchasing account after Minecraft Dungeons II launches on September 29, 2026.

If you are gifting the game, confirm the store’s gifting rules before purchase. The recipient must redeem or own the qualifying purchase on the account and platform where they plan to play.

# Purchase and Pre-Order Help

## You Were Not Charged Yet

Some storefronts do not charge for a pre-order immediately. Billing timing and payment authorization are controlled by the store where the order was placed. Check the order status, payment method, and expected charge date in that store’s purchase history.

## You See an Error While Pre-Ordering

If a pre-order page does not load or returns an error, verify that the game is available for pre-order in your region and on your selected platform, then try the purchase again from the platform’s store. If the Nintendo eShop does not show the pre-order, refresh the store listing and confirm you are viewing the correct regional store and console version.

## Your Cosmetics Are Missing

Confirm that you launched the game with the account and platform used for the qualifying purchase. Then check the Collectibles tab in the in-game inventory. Also verify that the pre-order or Deluxe content appears in the platform’s order history or installed add-ons. If the entitlement is missing from the order, use the purchase-help options provided by that storefront.

## Canceling a Pre-Order

Pre-order cancellation and refund rules vary by platform and retailer. Use the order-management or refund options for the store where the purchase was made.

# Additional Information

Detailed gameplay mechanics, and any content beyond the announced editions and DLC will be communicated through official Minecraft channels when that information is available.
