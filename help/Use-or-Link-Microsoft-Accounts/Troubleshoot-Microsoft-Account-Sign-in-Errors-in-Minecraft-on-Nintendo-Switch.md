If you [Sign In to Minecraft with a Microsoft Account on Nintendo Switch](./Sign-in-to-Minecraft-with-a-Microsoft-Account-on-Nintendo-Switch.md) and receive an error message when signing in, there are two common causes:

- You signed out of your Microsoft account but did not unlink it from your Nintendo Account.
- The Microsoft account you entered is linked to a different Nintendo Account.

Here are troubleshooting steps for the two error messages.

# If the Microsoft Account is already linked to a different Nintendo Account:

## Oops! 

## THAT MICROSOFT account has already been linked to another Nintendo Account. If you're the account owner, sign in to the previously connected Nintendo account.

**Issue**: The Microsoft account you are trying to sign in with is already linked to a different Nintendo Account/device.

**Solution**: Use a different Microsoft account that is not connected to a Nintendo Account.

- **Use a Microsoft account that is not connected with a Nintendo Account**:
  - Select **Try Again** and **Sign in with a Microsoft account**.
  - Sign in with a new or alternative Microsoft account to your Nintendo Account. See [How to create a new Microsoft account](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/account-billing/how-to-create-a-new-microsoft-account-a84675c3-3e9e-17cf-2911-3d56b15c0aaf).
- **Sign in to the Nintendo Account that’s linked to your Microsoft account and launch Minecraft on it:**
- Start Minecraft on your Nintendo Switch.
- Select **Settings** on the Minecraft launch screen.
- Select the **Account** tab.
- Select **Unlink Microsoft account**.
- Confirm that you want to unlink accounts.
- Select **Unlink**.
- **Visit Help Page:**
  - For more information on how to [Sign In to Minecraft with a Microsoft Account on Nintendo Switch](./Sign-in-to-Minecraft-with-a-Microsoft-Account-on-Nintendo-Switch.md)

# If the Nintendo Account is already linked to a different Microsoft Account:

## Oops! This account is already connected. Please sign in with your previously connected Microsoft Account. If you want to use a different one, you’ll need to unlink the current account first.

**Issue**: The Nintendo Account is already linked to a different Microsoft account.

**Solution**: Sign in with the associated Microsoft account or unlink your previous Microsoft account.

- **Sign in with the associated Microsoft account:**
  - Select **Try Again** and **Sign in with a Microsoft account.**
  - [Find your Microsoft account email address](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/account-profile/manage-account/forgot-microsoft-account-solution) if you don’t know the email address associated with the gamertag.
- **Unlink the Microsoft account**:
  - Select **Unlink Microsoft account**. This will unlink your Microsoft account from the other Nintendo Account.
  - Select **Sign in with a Microsoft account** and sign in with your Microsoft account.
- **Visit Help Page:**
  - For more information on how to [Sign In to Minecraft with a Microsoft Account on Nintendo Switch](./Sign-in-to-Minecraft-with-a-Microsoft-Account-on-Nintendo-Switch.md)

**Purchases stay with the original account:** Your Minecraft Store purchases made on your Nintendo account stay on your Nintendo account.

**Microsoft account benefits end:** If you do not sign in with another Microsoft account after you unlink, you will lose access to Minecraft Store purchases made on other platforms, cross-platform play, and access to Realms.
