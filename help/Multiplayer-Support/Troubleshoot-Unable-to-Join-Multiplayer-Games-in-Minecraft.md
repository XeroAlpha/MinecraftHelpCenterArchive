When you try to join another player's Minecraft world online, you may get a “**Multiplayer is disabled”** error or be unable to connect. Try these simple suggestions to fix common Minecraft multiplayer problems.

## Update your game version

Outdated Minecraft versions are a common cause of problems. Your game may not have automatically updated, and these steps will usually solve that:

- **Enable automatic updates**: Check your game platform settings to ensure you have the latest version.
- **Select latest release from the launcher**: Make sure you’re not using preview or snapshot.
- **Manually update your game**: See these articles for more help:
  - [Manually Update Minecraft on Mobile Devices](../Download-Install/Manually-Update-Minecraft-on-Mobile-Devices.md)
  - [Manually Update Minecraft on Consoles](../Download-Install/Manually-Update-Minecraft-on-Consoles.md)
  - [Manually Update Minecraft on Windows](../Download-Install/Manually-Update-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition-on-PC.md)

If you can’t join another player’s world, make sure they also update to the latest version.

## Check permissions and subscriptions (Bedrock only)

- **Allow multiplayer for your account**: See [Managing Multiplayer Game Access for a Child Account in Minecraft](../Account-Settings/Managing-Multiplayer-Game-Access-for-a-Child-Account-in-Minecraft.md) to enable multiplayer. You may need to do this even if you don’t have a child account.
- **Enable multiplayer games in world settings**: The host player must **allow multiplayer games permission** for their world. See [Invite Friends to Your Minecraft: Bedrock Edition World](./Invite-Friends-to-Your-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition-World.md) for steps to enable this.
- **Subscribe to your platform’s premium online subscription**: If you’re on a console, subscribe to [Xbox Game Pass Core](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-game-pass) or [PlayStation Plus](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps-plus/) to play with friends. Nintendo Switch players must also subscribe to [Nintendo Switch Online](https://ec.nintendo.com/US/en/membership/) for online multiplayer.

## Check your network connection and server status

- **Confirm the host is online**: To play with a friend on their hosted server or their hosted world, they must be online.\*
- **Confirm Realms is online**: Minecraft Realms rarely has service issues, but you can check [PlayFab Status](https://status.playfab.com/) and [Xbox Live Status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status) for status updates.
- **Disable custom DNS and open ports**: Minecraft may conflict with custom DNS and port configurations, even if you can connect to other websites or games. See [Troubleshoot Minecraft Network Connection Errors](../Error-Code-Troubleshooting/Troubleshoot-Minecraft-Network-Connection-Errors.md).

\* *This does not apply to Minecraft Realms. Realms owners do not have to be online for guests to play.*
