You can link your Microsoft account to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 to enable cross-platform play, access Marketplace content, and sync your progress.

## Use your Microsoft account with Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2

1.  Start Minecraft on your Nintendo Switch 2.
2.  Select **Sign in with a Microsoft Account**.
3.  Obtain the **8-digit Linking Code** from your Switch 2 screen.
4.  Go to the [Remote Connection page](https://login.live.com/oauth20_remoteconnect.srf) on a web browser on your phone or PC.
5.  Enter the **8-digit code** from your Switch 2 into the web page and click **Next**.
6.  Sign in to the Microsoft account you want to link to your Switch 2.
7.  Confirm the account you want to use is correct.

# Troubleshooting

- Ensure you are using a **personal Microsoft account**, not a work or school account.
- If redirected to **Parental Controls**, follow the on-screen instructions to allow account linking.
- If the code does not work, restart Minecraft and request a new code.

For more help with Sign-in errors: [Troubleshoot Microsoft Account Sign-in Errors in Minecraft on Nintendo Switch](../Use-or-Link-Microsoft-Accounts/Troubleshoot-Microsoft-Account-Sign-in-Errors-in-Minecraft-on-Nintendo-Switch.md)

## Nintendo Switch Online Validation

Some online Minecraft features on your Switch 2 may require an active Nintendo Switch Online membership. If you’re having trouble accessing Realms or another online feature, confirm that the Nintendo Account signed in on the console has the appropriate Nintendo Switch Online membership.

If Microsoft account sign-in succeeds but an online feature remains unavailable, verify the Nintendo Account and Nintendo Switch Online membership separately.

## Subscription Validation

If you have purchased a Minecraft subscription or content but do not see it in Minecraft:

- Confirm you are using the Nintendo Account that owns the purchase.
- Confirm you are signed in to Minecraft with the expected Microsoft account.
- For Realms, confirm that the Nintendo Account has the required Nintendo Switch Online membership.
- Check whether the subscription is active through your Nintendo Account.

If the subscription appears active through Nintendo but Minecraft does not recognize it, contact Minecraft Support.
