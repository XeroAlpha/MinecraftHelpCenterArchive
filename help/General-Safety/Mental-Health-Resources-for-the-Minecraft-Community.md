**Important:** If you or someone you know might be in danger right now, including thoughts of suicide, self-harm, or a threat from another person, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line right away. If you're under 18, please also tell a parent, guardian, teacher, or another adult you trust.

## If you're talking to us right now

If you've told us you're having a hard time or thinking about hurting yourself, **thank you for telling us. That took courage, and we want you to get support.**

- **Not safe right now?** Call your local emergency number or call or text a crisis line below. In the US, **call or text 988** any time.
- **Struggling but not in immediate danger?** Free, confidential crisis counselors are available 24/7 by text, phone, or chat. You can stay anonymous.
- **Under 18?** Talk to an adult you trust. You could say: *"I'm having a really hard time and I need help. Can we talk?"*

You're not alone, and there are many resources you can use to get support.

**Your privacy:** We never share your personal information or mental health details with other players. We share information only when the law requires it because someone's life may be in immediate danger.

## Where to get help

Our community spans the globe, and this list doesn't include every country. If your country isn't listed, you can find a crisis line near you at [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com/) or [befrienders.org](https://befrienders.org/). In an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

## Emergency services

- **US, Canada, Mexico:** 911
- **UK:** 999 or 112
- **EU and most mobile phones:** 112
- **Australia:** 000
- **Brazil:** 190

## Crisis Helplines

**United States**

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** call or text 988. For Spanish, press 2.
- **Crisis Text Line:** text HOME to 741741

**Canada**

- **9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline:** call or text 988
- **Kids Help Phone** (under 20): call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868

**UK and Ireland**

- **Samaritans:** call 116 123
- **Shout (UK):** text SHOUT to 85258
- **Text About It (Ireland):** text HELLO to 50808
- **PAPYRUS HOPELINE247** (under 35): call 0800 068 4141

**Australia**

- **Lifeline:** call 13 11 14
- **Kids Helpline** (ages 5–25): call 1800 55 1800

For ongoing support such as counseling or therapy, these organizations can point you in the right direction. You can also search for *"mental health support near me."*

## Worried about another player?

If you think someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services or tell a trusted adult. Otherwise, reach out to them:

- **Listen first.** It's okay not to know what to say. Showing you care makes a difference.
- **Give them space to talk.** Ask questions and let them lead.
- **Be kind and patient,** even if you don’t fully understand.
- **Encourage professional help** and share this page.
- **Look after yourself, too.** You don't have to carry this alone. Lean on people you trust.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I report a player who talks about self-harm?** On Bedrock Edition, use the in-game report option. On Java Edition, use chat reporting from the Social Interactions screen. Reporting helps us know that someone may need support.

**I told Minecraft Support I'm struggling. What should I do next?** Thank you for reaching out. Please contact one of the organizations above. They're free and confidential, and they're there to listen. If you're in danger right now, contact emergency services or a crisis line.

**Is the crisis line part of Minecraft?** No. These are independent, trusted services. Your conversations with them stay private, and Minecraft never sees them.
