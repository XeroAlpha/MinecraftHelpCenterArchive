If you’re running Minecraft on your Nintendo Switch 2 device via backward compatibility and want to move to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2, we have you covered.

The Switch 1 version was backward-compatible, but Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 is a full port.

Worlds, progress, and builds from the original Switch 1 version will transfer to the Switch 2, which means you can start Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 with your existing worlds intact, without needing to recreate them.

# Moving from Nintendo Switch to Nintendo Switch 2

Any data you plan to move needs to first exist on your Nintendo Switch 2 device. You can move data from your Nintendo Switch to the Nintendo Switch 2 using [Nintendo’s official System Transfer](https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch-2/transfer-guide/?srsltid=AU7gw4X_aMdqy30CufKnB_ZDVad3g-n-s4yOKXqouz4D_CdoINz6EO3D) feature.

If you are transferring from Minecraft: Nintendo Switch Edition, first follow the steps here to move your data to Minecraft (Bedrock Version): [How to Transfer Worlds on Nintendo Switch](../../feedback/Gameplay-and-Support-Resources/How-to-Transfer-Worlds-on-Nintendo-Switch.md)

## Use Nintendo's System Transfer

1.  On your **Nintendo Switch**, go to **System Settings -\> Users -\> Transfer Your User Data**.
2.  Connect both consoles to the same Wi-Fi and keep them powered on during the transfer.
3.  Designate your **Nintendo Switch** as the **Source** and your **Nintendo Switch 2** as the **Target**.
4.  This will move your **entire user profile**, including all local Minecraft save data, to your Nintendo Switch 2.

# Migrating worlds to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2

Once your save data has been transferred to your Nintendo Switch 2 device, you can begin the process of migrating it to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2.

## Migrating all of your worlds at once

A one-time migration will occur the first time you launch Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 and save data is detected from a Nintendo Switch device. This occurs automatically and once completed will not occur again.

## Alternative: Sync Old Worlds

Alternatively, you can also use Sync Old Worlds option to individually migrate worlds to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2:

1.  Go to Settings.
2.  Select Storage.
3.  Scroll to the bottom and select Sync Old Worlds.
4.  Once synced, select Manage and you will see your old worlds to convert.

# Troubleshooting

## Where Are My Worlds?

If you’ve already completed the mass migration process, and are trying to find additional worlds to transfer, see [How to Transfer Worlds on Nintendo Switch](../../feedback/Gameplay-and-Support-Resources/How-to-Transfer-Worlds-on-Nintendo-Switch.md)

1.  Go to Settings.
2.  Select Storage.
3.  Scroll to the bottom and select Sync Old Worlds.
4.  Once synced, select Manage and you will see your old worlds to convert.

## My Worlds Didn't Transfer

If your worlds didn’t transfer successfully, the one-time mass migration process may have failed. If so, you may see one of the following messages:

1.  If some worlds were successfully migrated but not all, you will see a **Retry** button. Select **Retry** to attempt to finish the rest of the migration process.
2.  If you do not have enough storage space to transfer all of your worlds, you will be asked to clear the required space on your Nintendo device in order to finish the transfer. 
3.  If you see a miscellaneous error, you will be shown the error. Select **Retry** after resolving the issue.

## Where is My Marketplace Content?

If you’re having trouble finding your Marketplace content on your Switch 2, see [View Your Minecraft Marketplace Content](../Marketplace-Troubleshooting/View-Your-Minecraft-Marketplace-Content.md)

## I Can't Find My Profile

If Minecraft can’t find your profile on Switch 2, it’s usually due to Microsoft account linking issues, Xbox privacy settings, or login method problems. Here’s how you can fix it: [Nintendo Account Issues on Switch 2](../Account-Sign-in/Nintendo-Account-Issues-on-Switch-2.md)

## Can I Move Multiple Profiles?

Minecraft worlds are associated with individual Nintendo Accounts. The world migration process must be performed per profile/account.
