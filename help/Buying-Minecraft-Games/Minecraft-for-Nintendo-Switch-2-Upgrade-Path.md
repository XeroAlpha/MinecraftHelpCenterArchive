# If You Already Own a Digital Version of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch

If you already own a digital version of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch, you can upgrade to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 Standard Edition for free or purchase the Deluxe or Ultimate Collection at a discounted price for a limited time. See below for the digital version details:

[Standard Edition](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/minecraft-switch-2/)**:** Free until January 31, 2027 (\$29.99 USD after) 

[Deluxe Collection](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/minecraft-deluxe-collection-switch-2/)**:** \$9.99 USD until January 31, 2027 (\$39.99 USD after). 

- The Deluxe Collection includes three beloved add-ons: Celebration Food, Rescue Dogs, and Plenty O’Blocks, 3 exclusive Character Creator items, and 700 Minecoins.

[Ultimate Collection](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/minecraft-ultimate-collection-switch-2/)**:** \$19.99 USD until January 31, 2027 (\$49.99 after). 

- The Ultimate Collection includes five essential add-ons: Celebration Food, Rescue Dogs, Plenty O’Blocks, as well as Decocraft and Weapons + Tools, five exclusive Character Creator items, and 1000 Minecoins.

**Note:** All versions include the Super Mario™ Mash-Up Pack with Vibrant Visuals. Vibrant Visuals may not be available in some gameplay, including split-screen and when using certain Marketplace Worlds and content.

# If You Already Own a Physical Version of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch

If you already own a physical version of the game, you can upgrade to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 Deluxe Collection for 50% off!

# To Purchase or Upgrade to Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2

1.  In the Nintendo [Nintendo eShop](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/minecraft-switch-2/), search for Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 and select it.
2.  If you already own a digital version of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch, you can get a digital Minecraft Standard Edition for Nintendo Switch 2 for free or purchase the Deluxe or Ultimate Collection at a discount by January 31, 2027. For more details, see: [Minecraft Migration from Switch 1 to Switch 2](../Download-Install/Minecraft-Migration-from-Switch-1-to-Switch-2.md)
3.  Select the version you want, scroll to the bottom of the next page, and follow the pre-order process.
4.  Details will be sent to the email address registered to your Nintendo Account. If a child account is being used, this information will be sent to the parent or guardian’s Nintendo Account.
