Currently, switching between Realms subscription plans isn't as easy as we'd like it to be. If you want to upgrade to a different plan, you'll need to:

1.  Cancel your existing subscription.
2.  Purchase a new one.
3.  Manually move your worlds and update your member list.

We know this can be frustrating, and we're working on making upgrades easier and more seamless in the future.

If you'd like to move your Core Realm to Plus, you can do so in the Minecraft Launcher or from within the game. If you'd like to move to Realms Pro, you can subscribe through the [Minecraft website](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/store/realms-pro?tabs=%7B%22details%22%3A0%7D) or the Realms tab in the Minecraft Launcher.
