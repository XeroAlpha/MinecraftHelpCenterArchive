Unlock the full potential of Realms with more admin and creative control! Visit [Minecraft Realms Pro](https://minecraft.net/store/realms-pro) to learn more about subscription options and the features.

# How to Create a Pro Realm

1.  Go to [Minecraft Realms Pro](https://minecraft.net/store/realms-pro)
2.  Select **Subscribe to Realms Pro.**
    1.  If there is no **Subscribe to Realms Pro** button, and there is a **Manage Realms Pr**o button, then you already have a Pro Realm. Only one owned Pro Realm can be associated with your account.
3.  If you’re not already signed into your Microsoft account, sign in.
4.  Agree to the Terms & Conditions.
5.  Complete the purchase process.
6.  Open Minecraft: Bedrock Edition.
7.  Select **Play**.
8.  Go to the **Realms** tab, where you will see your new Pro Realm.

**Note:** If you let your Pro Realm expire, it will still be linked to your account. Expired Pro Realms will be deleted 18+ months after its expiration.

# How to Renew an Expired Pro Realm

To renew an expired Pro Realm before it is permanently deleted 18+ months after expiration, simply go through the purchase process explained above using the account that owned the expired Pro Realm.
