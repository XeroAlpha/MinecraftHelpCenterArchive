When a world is uploaded to Realms Pro, it becomes an Editor Project to enable the use of Minecraft Editor. For players, the Editor Project functions just like a regular world. 

When you launch into a Pro Realm as an Editor, the Editor Project treats you as a different user than when you enter as a player. This means:

- You’ll have a different spawn point in Editor than you do as a player.
- You’ll have different items in your inventory.
- You’ll be a different level as a player than you are as an Editor. Experience is not shared across your two profiles.
- You’ll have different Status Effects (from Potions, Beacons, etc.) than you do as a player.
- If you die in a Hardcore world as a player, you can still enter it as an Editor.

The Editor Project saves your Editor data, just like it saves your player data. Each time you launch into the Editor Project as an Editor, your spawn point, hotbar, etc. will be just as you left it in your last Editor session.

**Note:** If the world is reverted to a backup, your Editor data will be reverted to what it was when the backup was created. 

Just as you have separate player data for each world, you’ll have separate Editor data for each Editor Project.

- What you do as an Editor in Project A does not affect your experience as an Editor in Project B.

**Note:** You will not be able to earn achievements while Editing.
