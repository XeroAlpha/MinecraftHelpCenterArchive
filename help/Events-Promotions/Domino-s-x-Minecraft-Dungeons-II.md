For more information about the Domino's x Minecraft Dungeons II promotion, please visit our blog post [here](https://www.minecraft.net/article/dominos-minecraft-dungeons-ii).

**Note:** Any issues related to the code received from Domino's will need to be directed to [Domino's support](https://www.dominos.com/en/content/customer-support).

If you have questions about Minecraft, unrelated to the Domino's promotion, contact [Minecraft support](https://help.minecraft.net/).

## What are the Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II in-game items? 

The Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II in-game items are cosmetic downloadable items for Minecraft Dungeons II, which requires the game be purchased separately. The in-game items include the Domino’s Skin, Cheesy Cube (Pet), and the Slice Slayer Cape. 

## How can I earn the in-game items? 

Beginning October 26 in [select regions](#i-noticed-this-promotion-is-for-the-united-states-are-other-international-dominos-partnering-with-minecraft-dungeons-ii-as-well), when you order and prepay for the Domino™ online, you’ll earn 1 of 3 in-game items (awarded randomly). Eligible orders require Domino’s® Rewards membership. Simply log in when placing your order, or order and sign up after checkout (for free) and you’ll gain access to the codes you earned in the My Deals & Rewards section of [Dominos.com](https://nam06.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=http%3A%2F%2Fdominos.com%2F&data=05%7C02%7Cv-mdell%40microsoft.com%7Cc8865f830b6a4d9ee16d08df2ed9a866%7C72f988bf86f141af91ab2d7cd011db47%7C1%7C0%7C639281184497153153%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=i2vhuwjPf43HWg3rvodHuVz46yNspyD%2Fg79VC8Oh6Ho%3D&reserved=0) or the app. Limit 10 items per order. In-game items require Minecraft Dungeons II (sold separately). Limit 1 of each in-game item per Microsoft account. For more information, please review the [Terms and Conditions](https://www.dominos.com/minecraft-dungeons-ii/terms).

## Can I order in-store or pay with cash at the register to earn in-game items? 

No. All qualifying orders must be placed and paid for online or in the Domino’s app. 

## Do I have to be a Domino’s® Rewards member to qualify? 

Yes, but joining is completely free. If you don’t have an account yet, you can sign up after checkout to receive your in-game item code. 

## I ordered the Domino before October 26 and received a Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II box. Does that mean I can claim an in-game item? 

You may have found a collectible Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II box early, but in-game items are only available with qualifying orders placed on or after October 26, 2026. Purchases of the new Domino before October 26, 2026, do not qualify for entry into the 20,000 Game Copy Giveaway. 

## I received a Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II box, but I didn’t order the Domino. Do I still earn an in-game item code? 

No. You must place a qualifying online order for the Domino™ to receive an in-game item. 

## Is there a limit on how many in-game items I can earn? 

Customers can earn multiple in-game items in a single order by purchasing multiple Dominos (one random item per qualifying Domino). The maximum number of in-game items that can be earned in a single order is 10. In-game item codes are awarded at random and may result in duplicates of the same item.  In-game items require Minecraft Dungeons II (sold separately). 

## Can I choose which in-game items I receive? 

You are unable to choose your in-game item. Each qualifying Domino purchase generates a single, randomized code with equal odds. You may receive duplicates across multiple orders or when ordering multiple Dominos at once in the same order. Duplicate codes can be shared with friends and family for the ultimate co-op adventure. 

## I didn’t receive the code I wanted; can I swap it for another one? 

No. Unfortunately, codes cannot be swapped or traded in for alternate item types. 

## If I get duplicates of the same in-game item, can I share my codes with a friend? 

Yes! We encourage you to share the duplicate codes with friends for the ultimate co-op gaming experience. Please note that Codes are not available for resale. 

## Can I redeem an in-game code more than once? 

No. Each individual code can only be redeemed once.  In addition, multiple codes for the same in-game item cannot be redeemed to the same Microsoft account because there is a limit of one of each in-game item per Microsoft account. 

## I received my code, but I'm having issues redeeming my code or accessing my items in-game 

The Domino's x Minecraft Dungeons II DLC in-game items require the [Minecraft Dungeons II game](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/store/minecraft-dungeons-ii) (sold separately), Internet access, and a Microsoft account. We encourage you to review the redemption instructions below to ensure you have successfully completed the steps for redemption:

- Make a qualifying pre-paid online order for the new Domino. 
- In your Domino’s MyRewards account, locate your in-game item code under Domino’s MyDeals and Rewards. 
- Click the “Take Me There” button or visit [www.minecraft.net/dominos](https://www.minecraft.net/dominos) 

### Select your scenario:

### In-game:

To redeem your Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II in-game items, you need to own [Minecraft Dungeons II](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/store/minecraft-dungeons-ii). 

- Launch Minecraft Dungeons II and sign in with your Microsoft account. 
- From your title screen, select “Settings” then scroll down to 'Exclusive Content'
- Redeem Promotional Code 
- Enter the 12-character in-game item code (Codes must be redeemed by December 31, 2027)
- Enjoy!

## Is there a deadline to redeem codes? 

Yes, code(s) must be redeemed by December 31, 2027. They will stay in your Domino’s My Deals & Rewards section until February 7,2027. Beyond that date, you will need to document your codes elsewhere as codes will no longer be visible in your account, so we recommend you write them down. 

## How do I get a collectible Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II box? 

All purchases of the Domino™ during the promotional timeframe (October 26, 2026 to January 10, 2027) will come in a collectible box, while supplies last.  

## How many collectible Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II boxes are there?  

There are six (6) collectible Domino's x Minecraft Dungeons II pizza boxes. Boxes are distributed randomly with each qualifying order of the new Domino, while supplies last. 

## Can I request a specific box or try to swap my box for the one I want? 

Boxes are distributed randomly, while supplies last. Boxes are not for sale and cannot be traded in for different boxes. 

## Can I purchase a collectible box or ask to receive just the box for free? 

No. You must purchase the Domino™ to receive a Domino’s x Minecraft Dungeons II box.
