Minecraft Realms automatically creates a backup save of your world at regular intervals. This allows you to restore your world if something goes wrong.

# Automatic Saves

We automatically save your progress:

- Every 30 minutes
- When you select Save & Quit in the menu screen  
- Any time you disconnect from your Realm.

We can’t keep all of your saves around forever, so the following retention policy is in place:

- The **four most recent saves** are retained.
- **In the last 24 hours:** We retain one save every 4 hours.
- **1 day to 8 days ago:** We retain one save per day.
- **9 days to 36 days ago:** We retain one save per week.
- **37 days to 121 days ago:** We retain one save per month.
- **122+ days ago:** No saves are retained.

A Realm world is downloadable within 18 months of your subscription expiring, but the world backups associated with that Realm will be deleted 30 days after your subscription expires.

# Manual Saves on Minecraft: Bedrock Edition Realms

Create manual saves of your Realm world in Minecraft: Bedrock Edition to store a backup copy of your Realm whenever you want.  Manual saves remain in your Realm storage until you delete them, giving you full control over what to keep. 

Each Bedrock Realms tier (Core, Plus, and Pro) comes with a different amount of manual save storage. If your active world is larger than the storage space you have available, you won't be able to create manual saves. However, Realms will continue to save your world automatically as described above.

## Create a Manual Save

1.  Launch Minecraft and select **Play**.
2.  Select the **Realms** tab.
3.  Select the **Realms Hub** for the Realm you want to manually save.
4.  Select the **World** tab. Then, select **Saves**.
5.  Select **Save active world**.

## Delete a Manual Save 

1.  Launch Minecraft.
2.  Select **Play.**
3.  Select the **Realms **tab.
4.  Select the **Realms Hub** for the Realm you want to manually save.
5.  Select the **World** tab. Then, select **Saves**.
6.  Select the **kebob menu button **(three dots) to the right of the save file you wish to delete.
7.  Select **Delete Save**.
