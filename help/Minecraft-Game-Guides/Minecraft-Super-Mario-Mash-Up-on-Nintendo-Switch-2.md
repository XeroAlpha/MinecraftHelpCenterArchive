Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2 includes the Super Mario Mash-Up Pack, now with Vibrant Visuals! Explore a Mario-themed world featuring iconic locations like Peach’s Castle and Delfino Plaza, themed textures, 40 player skins, and 15 tracks inspired by the Super Mario series. Head to the Marketplace to claim your included pack and start a new world. 

## How do I access the new Super Mario Mash-Up Pack:

- All players need to manually claim the Super Mario Mash-Up Pack from the Marketplace by searching for “**Super Mario Mash-Up**” and claiming it for free.
- For players that are migrating a Mario World (or have an active Mario skin equipped) from Switch 1, they will get a prompt when entering the **Worlds List Screen** which automatically directs them to the Marketplace page to acquire the pack.

## Why Aren’t I Seeing the New Vibrant Visuals Experience in the Super Mario Mashup Pack?

- Split-screen disables Vibrant Visuals in all gameplay modes, including while playing the Super Mario Mash-Up Pack. To access Vibrant Visuals, you need to play in single-player mode.

## Why Can’t My Nintendo Online or Xbox Live Friend Join Me in My Super Mario Mash-Up World?

- The Super Mario Mash-Up world has cross-play disabled, meaning only other players on Nintendo Switch 2 can play together in online multiplayer. Switch 1 players or non-Nintendo players cannot join.

**Note:** This is the same way the original Mario world performs on Switch 1.
