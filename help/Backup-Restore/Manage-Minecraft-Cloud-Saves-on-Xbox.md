Xbox automatically saves your most recent Minecraft worlds in the cloud, so you’re less likely to lose your progress. Here’s how to check your cloud storage, see which worlds aren’t backed up, and free up space when it runs low.

## Enable automatic cloud saves on Xbox

Cloud saves are tied to your Microsoft account. When your Xbox is online and you’re signed in, Minecraft backs up your worlds automatically.

New worlds are backed up as soon as you create them. Worlds you created offline are backed up the first time you play them. Worlds you don’t play will only exist on your device until you back them up.

To protect an older world right away, upload it from **Settings** \> **Storage** \> **Manage Worlds**.

## Review and manage cloud storage on Xbox

On the Play screen, you can see the status of each of your worlds:

- **Cloud-synced**: Saved on this device and in the cloud. These worlds have no icon in the top-right corner.
- **Cloud-only**: Saved in the cloud but not on this device. These worlds have a cloud icon and can be downloaded to your device.
- **Local-only**: Saved on this device but not in the cloud. These worlds have a hard drive icon with an up arrow.

To check your storage and choose which worlds are backed up, go to **Settings** \> **Storage** \> **Manage Worlds** in Minecraft. The storage summary shows your cloud status and how much space you’re using, so you can tell when you’re getting close to full.

Select the info button next to a world to see its details, including file size, last played date, and where it’s stored. From there you can:

- **Upload to cloud**: back up a local-only world.
- **Download from cloud**: bring a cloud-only world, or a world from another device, to this device.
- **Delete local world**: remove the copy on this device.
- **Remove from cloud**: delete the cloud copy but keep the world on this device.

If your cloud storage is running low, or is full, Minecraft will show a message pointing you to **Manage Worlds**. Move less important worlds to local-only storage, or remove them from the cloud, to make room for the worlds you want synced across your Xbox devices.

## Restore a cloud save on Xbox

Xbox restores your most recent cloud save automatically if files are missing on your device. If the save on your device is out of date, delete it and pull the latest version from the cloud:

1.  Press the **Home** button on your Xbox controller and go to **My games & apps**.
2.  Select **See all** and choose Minecraft from your games list.
3.  Press the **Menu** button and select **Manage game**.
4.  Select **Saved data**, choose your profile, and select **Delete from console**.
5.  Launch Minecraft to sync with the cloud and download your latest save.
