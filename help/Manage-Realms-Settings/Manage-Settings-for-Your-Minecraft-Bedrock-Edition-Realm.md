*For Minecraft: Java Edition, see* [*Manage Your Minecraft: Java Edition Realm*](./Manage-Settings-for-Your-Minecraft-Java-Edition-Realm.md)*.*

Minecraft: Bedrock Edition Realm owners and admins can change the Realm name and description, manage player settings, and alter various additional settings through the Realms menu.

# Access the Manage Realm options from the Play Menu

1.  Launch Minecraft and select **Play**.
2.  Select the **Realms** tab.
3.  Select the **Realms Hub **of the realm you want to manage. Once you access Realms Hub, you will see the **Community**,** Timeline,** **World**, and **Settings **tabs. 

# Manage realm options

## Community

### STORY FEED

- **Moderate your Realm’s Story feed:** Select the **Options **for a story post to Delete, Report, or Dismiss the post.

### MEMBERS

- **Invite players to your Realm**: See [Invite Friends to Your Minecraft: Bedrock Edition Realm](../Create-or-Join-Realms/Invite-Friends-to-Your-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition-Realm.md).
- **Manage players in your Realm**: See [Moderate Your Minecraft Realm](./Moderate-Your-Minecraft-Realm.md).

### ADMIN LOG

- **View Admin Log**: Admins and Owners of a realm can track changes and updates made to the Realm. 

## Timeline

The Realms Timeline shows when each member of the Realm was active. The following options are available if you select the kebab menu to the left of the screen, above the Timeline member list:

- About Timeline
- View Opted-Out Members
- Opt out from Timeline

## World

From the World tab, manage your realm worlds and your world’s save data:

### SLOTS 

**Edit World**

- **See **[Change or Edit Your Minecraft: Bedrock Edition Realm World](../Manage-Realms-Worlds/Change-or-Edit-Your-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition-Realm-World.md) for a breakdown of Edit World options.

**World Options**

- **Download World**: Your world will be downloaded and added to your Worlds tab. See [Download a Realms World in Minecraft: Bedrock Edition](../Manage-Realms-Save-Files/Download-a-Realms-World-in-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition.md) for more information.
- **Replace World: **This option will remove your current world from your Realm’s active slot and let you choose a world to replace it with. 
- **Reset World: **This will remove your current world from your Realm’s active slot and replace it with a brand-new world.

### SAVES

See [How We Backup Your Minecraft Realms World](../Manage-Realms-Save-Files/How-We-Backup-Your-Minecraft-Realms-World.md) and [Find Your Realms World Saves in Minecraft: Bedrock Edition](../Manage-Realms-Save-Files/Find-Your-Realms-World-Saves-in-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition.md) for more information.

## Settings

### HUB

- **Badge Notifications**: Choose whether to show badge notifications for unread stories.
- **Real Events**: Allow Minecraft to post stories for gameplay accomplishments. Enabling this will require the realm to restart.
- **Realm Event Coordinates**: Display coordinates for certain Realm Events if the world's Show Coordinates setting was enabled when the events were triggered.
- **Require Opt in to Timeline: **Require everyone to opt into the Timeline before they can play on the Realm.
- **Timeline**: Allow everyone to view the timeline.

### SERVER

- **Choose your Realm server location:** [Set Your Minecraft: Bedrock Edition Realm Server Region Preference](./Set-Your-Minecraft-Bedrock-Edition-Realm-Server-Region-Preference.md).
- **Temporarily Close Realm**: You can temporarily close your Realm, preventing play while you change settings. Reopen it when you’re ready. See [Close and Reopen Your Minecraft Realm to Solve Connection Errors](../Troubleshoot-Minecraft-Realms/Close-and-Reopen-Your-Minecraft-Realm-to-Solve-Connection-Errors.md).
- **Player Limit:** The number of players that can play on the Realm at the same time. This setting is only adjustable on Realms Pro subscriptions, where it defaults to 20, but can go as high as 50.
- **Render Distance Limit:** How far Realm members can see. They can adjust their own render distance but will not see past this limit. Higher render distances may not be visible on some devices. This setting can go up to 25 chunks for Realms Core and Realms Plus. On Realms Pro, it defaults to 40 chunks but can go up to 96 chunks.
- **Simulation Distance:** The game loads and applies changes within a range of 64x64 blocks from the player. This setting is only adjustable on Realms Pro subscriptions where it defaults to 4 chunks but can go up to 12 chunks. This setting is adjustable in 2-chunk increments (4, 6, 8, 10, & 12).

**Note:** If you update the Player limit, Render distance limit, or Simulation distance while the server is online, the change will not take effect immediately - it will take effect the next time the server boots up. 

To force a reboot, you can temporarily close the Realm with the "Temporarily close Realm" toggle and then reopen it. However, closing the Realm will disconnect anyone currently playing.

### REALM INFO

- **Change the Realm name and description**: These are publicly visible to invited players.
- **Manage the Recipes Unlock feature**: Manage whether players can unlock crafting recipes by only collecting related items.

### SUBSCRIPTION (Accessible only to the Realm Owner)

- **See subscription details**: Your Realm size and remaining subscription time will be listed.
- **Manage your subscription**: See our [Manage Realms Subscriptions](https://help.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/sections/26104341937421) section.

You can also manage your Realm on Minecraft.net. See [Edit your Realm on Minecraft.net](./Edit-your-Realms-on-Minecraft-net.md).
